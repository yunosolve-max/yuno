What happens when a customer scans

From the first scan to the paid bill, everyone sees the same order, live.

01/ 04

Scan and order

  1. Scan and orderThe menu opens on their phone with photos and prices. They tap + and place the order.
  2. Kitchen acceptsThe counter and kitchen ring. Either one taps Accept and picks a ready time.
  3. Cook and serveThe kitchen taps Ready. The customer sees it on their phone, and plays games while they wait.
  4. Bill and doneOne tap prints the bill. Daily sales and the calendar update by themselves.
