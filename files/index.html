From scan to paid bill in 4 steps

No waiting for a waiter to bring the menu, no shouting orders across the room.

  1. ScanThe customer scans the QR sticker on their table. The menu opens in the browser.
  2. OrderThey pick dishes, add a note like “less sugar” and send it.
  3. CookThe kitchen phone buzzes. They accept, set a ready time and cook.
  4. Serve and billWaiters get “ready” alerts. One tap saves the GST bill and prints it.
